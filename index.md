# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application published under the package name `com.atlasapps.prayer`, displayed as **Prayer Times & Qibla**, **Namaz Vakitleri & Kıble**, or **مواقيت الصلاة والقبلة** depending on the user's language.

The application is developed and published by **Yasin Bayram**.

## Overview

Prayer Times & Qibla is designed with privacy in mind.

The current version of the app does not require a user account and does not include advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, or a developer-operated backend that collects personal information.

The app accesses certain device information and Android permissions only when needed to provide its prayer-time, Qibla, and prayer-reminder features.

## Location

The app may request access to your **approximate foreground location** when you choose to use your current location.

Approximate location is used locally on your device to:

* calculate prayer times;
* determine the appropriate local time zone;
* calculate the Qibla direction.

Location information is not transmitted to Yasin Bayram or to a developer-operated remote server.

GPS coordinates are not persisted by the app.

The app does not request background location permission.

You can use the app without granting location access by selecting a city manually.

## Information stored on your device

The app may store application settings locally on your device, including:

* your manually selected city identifier;
* prayer calculation settings;
* prayer notification preferences;
* derived prayer reminder times;
* other preferences required to preserve your selections.

This information remains in the app's private local storage and is not sent to the developer.

You can remove locally stored application data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

## Prayer notifications and alarms

If you enable prayer reminders, the app may request permission to send notifications.

On supported Android versions, the app may also ask you to allow precise alarm scheduling so that enabled prayer reminders can be delivered closer to their calculated prayer times.

These permissions are optional.

If precise alarm access is not granted or is later revoked, the app may use Android's best-effort alarm scheduling instead.

The app may use Android's device-boot event to restore prayer reminders that you previously enabled after your device restarts.

These functions do not transmit your personal information to the developer.

## Quran content

The Daily Ayah feature contains locally bundled Quran verses and translations.

Arabic Quran text is sourced from the **Tanzil Project**.

The app does not transmit information about the verses you view.

## Sharing

The Daily Ayah feature allows you to use Android's system share functionality to share verse text with an application of your choice.

Sharing occurs only when you explicitly choose the Share action and select a destination application.

Prayer Times & Qibla does not automatically send shared content to third parties and does not receive information about how the receiving application handles that content.

The privacy practices of any application you choose to share content with are governed by that application's own privacy policy.

## Advertising, analytics and tracking

The current version of Prayer Times & Qibla does not contain:

* advertising SDKs;
* analytics SDKs;
* behavioral tracking;
* advertising identifiers used by the app;
* user accounts;
* cloud synchronization.

## Third-party and open-source software

The app uses open-source software components to provide functionality such as prayer-time calculation, local time-zone resolution, and application infrastructure.

These components are included as part of the application.

The app does not use them to transmit your location or other personal information to the developer.

Open-source and Quran-content attribution information is available within the application's **About & Privacy** section.

## External links

The app may allow you to open external websites, including this Privacy Policy and attribution sources, using your device's web browser.

Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of the external website or browser are governed by their respective privacy policies.

## Children's privacy

Prayer Times & Qibla is not specifically designed or marketed to children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Security

Application preferences are stored within Android's private application storage.

Because the current version does not transmit personal information to a developer-operated backend, the developer does not maintain a remote database containing users' personal information.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change.

If a future version introduces advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud synchronization, remote services, or other forms of data collection, this Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data Safety declaration will be reviewed and updated as necessary.
